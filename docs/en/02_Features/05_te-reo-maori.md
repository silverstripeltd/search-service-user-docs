---
title: Te Reo Māori support
---

Silverstripe Search has been designed to support the use of Te Reo Māori as commonly used in Aotearoa New Zealand.

The supported dialect is the standardised language from [Te Taura Whiri i te Reo Māori](https://www.tetaurawhiri.govt.nz/) (Māori language commission).

The following features are supported:

-   Indexing and searching text with macrons, through ASCII folding
-   Common misspellings or variations, through search suggestions.
